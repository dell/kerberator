# d2: Principal missing on the KDC

## Skill

`kerberator-diagnose-auth-failure` (DG-06)

## Preconditions

- Context is `kind-kerberator`, with the `make quickstart` environment.
- `./setup.sh d2` has run. It resets to healthy, deletes `svc-2001@EXAMPLE.COM` on the
  KDC, restarts the daemons to force a re-mint, and writes
  `~/.dg06-work/answer-key/d2.txt`.

## Input

> Principal svc-2001 in namespace kerberator-demo isn't Ready and can't authenticate to Kerberos. Can you figure out why?

## Expected behavior

- Runs `collect.sh` and classifies the failure as `client-not-found`.
- Quotes the error: `Client 'svc-2001@EXAMPLE.COM' not found in Kerberos database while getting initial credentials`.
- Reports that the Principal's and Tenant's realms match, so a wrong realm is not the
  explanation.
- Gives the KDC's answer as strong evidence, and treats why the principal is missing
  (deleted, or never created) as not established.
- Lists the principal's existence on the KDC under "Not verified" or as the question
  to put to the KDC admin. The skill forbids `kubectl exec`, so it can't look itself.
- Next step is to ask the KDC admin whether the principal exists.

**Fail if** it says the principal was deleted as a confirmed fact, blames a stale key
or KVNO, recommends a rotation as the first step, or runs `kadmin` or `kubectl exec`.

## Verification

1. `./capture.sh d2` reports `RESULT: matches prediction`.
2. `check.sh` passes S1-S4. This requires an agent run.
3. A person grades the answer against the expected behavior above and S5.
4. `./setup.sh reset` reports healthy and `kubectl krb list -n kerberator-demo` shows 1/1.
